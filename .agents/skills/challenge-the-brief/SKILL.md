# Challenge the brief

1. Restate the problem in two sentences and ask if that is right.
2. Ask one question at a time. Cover: who uses it, what they see, where the data comes from, scale, cost limit, what must never happen, what happens when a provider fails.
3. Point out anything in the brief that looks weak, missing or risky.
4. Stop when the problem is clear. Say "The problem is clear" and summarise it in five bullets.
5. Do not propose a solution or write code in this step.

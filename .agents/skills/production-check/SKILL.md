# Production check

Propose daily checks and alert thresholds for:
- Answers collected per model compared with expected
- Empty or failed response rate
- Sudden swings in mention rate for any brand
- Cost per model per day, alert at 80% of budget
- A hand-checked sample of 20 answers to confirm brand extraction is still accurate

For each check, say what the alert means and the first thing to investigate.
